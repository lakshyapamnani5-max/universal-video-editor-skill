# Editorial Motion Graphics

Use for presenter-led explainers, product promos and creator videos with polished motion design.

## Look
- Warm off-white or cream canvas.
- Deep charcoal typography with one vivid accent.
- Oversized editorial headlines, strike-throughs and underline emphasis.
- Rounded presenter panel, usually lower third or lower half.
- Floating UI cards, charts, timelines and infographic elements.
- Strong negative space and restrained shadows.

## Motion
- Headlines slide or wipe in with short easing.
- Accent words reveal after the main phrase.
- Cards enter with subtle scale and stagger.
- Charts draw on; counters animate only from real supplied values.
- Use short whooshes/clicks for meaningful transitions only.
- Avoid constant movement and generic transition packs.

## Scene rhythm
1. Hook: bold headline and presenter.
2. Context: one visual metaphor or simple UI card.
3. Explanation: one chart/diagram per key idea.
4. Proof: source-grounded number or product demonstration.
5. Close: concise CTA and logo lockup.

## Presenter
Keep face unobstructed. Use consistent crop, comfortable headroom and stable placement. If the presenter layout moves, make the move intentional and synchronized with a scene change.
